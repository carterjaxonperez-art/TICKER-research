## What the company is

Eli Lilly and Company is a global pharmaceutical company operating as one reportable segment. It discovers, develops, manufactures, markets, and sells pharmaceutical products worldwide.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 1, p. 62.

## What is going well

Lilly reported $65.179 billion of 2025 revenue, up 45% from $45.043 billion in 2024. Its MD&A attributes the consolidated increase primarily to a 50% volume contribution, partly offset by a 6% negative price contribution.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A, p. 48.

Mounjaro generated $22.965 billion of 2025 revenue, up 99% year over year, and Zepbound generated $13.542 billion, up 175%. Together, the two products accounted for about 56% of 2025 revenue (calculation: $36.507 billion divided by $65.179 billion).  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A, pp. 48–49; calculation shown.

The company reported 2025 net income of $20.640 billion and operating cash flow of $16.8 billion.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p. 57; Item 7 MD&A, p. 51.

## What could go wrong

The 2025 filing says Mounjaro and Zepbound drove both higher volume and lower realized prices in the United States.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A, pp. 48–49.

At December 31, 2025, Lilly reported $42.503 billion of total debt and $7.268 billion of cash and cash equivalents. It also reported $7.8 billion of capital expenditures in 2025 and said capital expenditures would be meaningfully higher in the near term as it invests in manufacturing capacity.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 59; Item 8, Note 11—Debt, p. 76; Item 7 MD&A, pp. 50–51.

Lilly disclosed product-liability litigation related to incretin medicines, including Mounjaro, Trulicity, and Zepbound. The disclosure reports allegations and proceedings; it does not establish an outcome or quantify a final loss.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 16—Legal and Regulatory Matters, p. 90.

## Performance against history

Lilly's revenue rose from $28.541 billion in 2022 to $65.179 billion in 2025.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2022 Form 10-K, Item 8, Note 2—Revenue, p. 62; Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A, p. 48.

The intervening FY2024 10-K reported revenue of $45.043 billion, up 32% from 2023. Management said that 2024 growth was driven by increased volume and, to a lesser extent, higher realized prices; it identified Mounjaro and Zepbound among the primary drivers.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2024 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A, pp. 41, 47–48.

The 2022 filing also described revenue declines from COVID-19 antibodies after emergency authorization ended and from Alimta after generic competition.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2022 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A, pp. 46–47.

## Outlook and open questions

The FY2025 filing states that Lilly's long-term success depends on its ability to maintain or strengthen its competitive position and deliver further innovations as the therapeutic landscape evolves.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A, p. 47.

The latest Q2 2026 10-Q reports $22.974 billion of quarterly revenue, up 48% from Q2 2025. Its MD&A attributes the increase primarily to higher volume, partly offset by lower realized prices, principally for Mounjaro and Zepbound.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Item 2 MD&A, pp. 24, 28–29.

In its August 5, 2026 earnings release, Lilly raised 2026 revenue guidance to $85–$87 billion. This is management guidance rather than an independent forecast.  
**Source:** Eli Lilly Q2 2026 earnings release, August 5, 2026, p. 3.
