# Lab 08 — Eli Lilly Deal Evidence and Valuation Triangulation

**Target and valuation date:** Eli Lilly (LLY), September 9, 2026. The closing price was $1,124.21.

## Peer policy

I admit profitable, U.S.-listed operating biopharma companies with human-medicine product sales and GAAP annual diluted EPS. I qualify candidates with different therapeutic concentration, product mix, patent exposure, or other operating segments; I do not select a peer because its multiple produces a preferred price.

| Candidate | Decision | Business fit and difference | Annual EPS public by date | Same-date price |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Merck (MRK) | Qualify | Human-medicine business with oncology and cardiometabolic products; Animal Health and KEYTRUDA concentration differ from Lilly's incretin-driven mix. | FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS $7.28; published Feb. 3, 2026. | $147.53 |
| Amgen (AMGN) | Qualify | Profitable human-medicine operating company; its established portfolio and therapeutic mix differ from Lilly's incretin concentration and growth profile. | FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS $14.23; published Feb. 3, 2026. | $391.27 |

Lilly FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS was $22.95, published February 4, 2026.

## Peer P/E result

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Merck P/E | 20.265110× |
| Amgen P/E | 27.496135× |
| Median P/E | 23.880622× |
| Lilly implied range | $465.08–$631.04 |
| Lilly at peer median | $548.06 |
| Remove Amgen | $465.08; change −$82.98 |

The higher-multiple Amgen removal lowers the median-implied value. With one peer remaining, the result is a reference estimate rather than a range.

## DCF comparison and judgment

| Method | Result and date | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Week 3 DCF | $352.48 per share, September 9, 2026 | Years 2–5 growth, WACC, and terminal growth remain placeholders. |
| Peer P/E | $465.08–$631.04; median $548.06, September 9, 2026 | Both peers are qualified, not identical; annual GAAP EPS periods and business mixes differ. |

**Provisional call: watch-defer.** Both methods are below the $1,124.21 close, but I will not average them. I would reconsider if sourced long-run Lilly growth assumptions improve materially or the price moves into the P/E range; I will monitor Lilly's operating cash flow less capex and the durability of incretin demand.

**Skeptical question:** Are the 6%, 5%, 4%, and 3% DCF growth assumptions defensible for Lilly's post-2026 product mix, or are they merely inherited training placeholders? **Judgment: accept.** This is the weakest supported DCF assumption and could materially change the conclusion.
